DocKit Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Files and processing

DocKit processes the photos, signatures, and document images you select on your device. The app does not upload them to a DocKit server. Finished JPEG and PDF files are saved in Work Gallery on your device. You choose where to save additional exported copies. If you choose a cloud storage provider through Android's file picker, that provider handles the file under its own privacy policy.

Collection and sharing

DocKit does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics. The app does not collect or share personal data with its developer.

Retention and deletion

Android or the file picker may keep temporary copies in the app cache. Clear DocKit's cache in Android settings to remove those copies. Delete finished files from Work Gallery or clear app storage in Android settings. Delete original files and exported copies from the file manager or storage provider where they are saved.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email on DocKit's Google Play listing.
